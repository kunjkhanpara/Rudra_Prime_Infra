SERVICES

From site assessment to long-term performance.

Turnkey renewable-energy services for rooftop, industrial, ground-mounted and wind-energy opportunities.

01 / SOLAR EPC

Solar systems engineered for the way you use energy.

Our solar EPC offering covers the project lifecycle: feasibility, system design, procurement coordination, installation, testing, commissioning and support.

  • Rooftop solar for homes, offices and institutions
  • Commercial & industrial rooftop systems
  • Ground-mounted solar plants
  • On-grid, hybrid and selected off-grid applications
  • Electrical and grid-connection coordination
02 / WIND ENERGY

Supporting wind projects across the development cycle.

Wind projects require land, access, documentation, surveys, electrical planning and field coordination. Our demo service model is structured to support those workstreams.

  • Land and documentation coordination
  • Site access and field support
  • Project documentation assistance
  • Electrical and infrastructure coordination
  • Execution and vendor coordination
03 / ENGINEERING & ELECTRICAL

Build the infrastructure behind reliable generation.

Renewable plants depend on well-planned electrical and civil infrastructure. Services can include preliminary design, cable routing, HT/LT coordination, protection, evacuation and construction support.

  • Site and load assessment
  • Electrical single-line and layout coordination
  • HT/LT and cable-route planning
  • Substation and evacuation coordination
  • Quality inspection and commissioning support
04 / O&M

Protect performance after commissioning.

Our O&M model can include scheduled inspections, cleaning coordination, fault reporting, inverter checks, thermography coordination and performance reviews.

  • Preventive maintenance schedules
  • Plant inspection and housekeeping
  • Performance and generation review
  • Fault escalation and service coordination
  • Annual maintenance planning
